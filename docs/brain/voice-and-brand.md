# Voice and brand

Recorded 2026-11-15 from founder feedback and current agency design guidance. Apply these as editorial defaults; an explicit brief or existing product design system controls the task.

## Voice

- Clear, personal, professional, and direct.
- Lead with the benefit or decision. Explain technical details when they help the reader.
- Prefer concrete actions to abstract technology claims.
- Remove redundant labels and unnecessary slogans.
- Distinguish ambition from evidence. Use specifics rather than unsupported superlatives.
- Explain access in plain language: who shares which information, and who can see it.

Example rewrites below are starting points, not approved marketing copy:

| Less clear | Clearer |
| --- | --- |
| “A permissioned intelligence ecosystem” | “Clubs and players choose which data to share, and with whom.” |
| “A comprehensive real-time intelligence platform” | “One place to connect contracts, scouting information, and club relationships.” |
| “Leverage AI to optimize career outcomes” | “Use the available evidence to compare a player's next options.” |

## Design judgment

The desired experience is sleek, intuitive, and easy to navigate. Preserve the existing design when asked for a narrow edit. Do not fill empty space with unnecessary words or decoration.
